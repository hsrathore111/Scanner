Privacy policy

Last updated LAST_UPDATED. Operated by OPERATOR_NAME.

What we collect

  • Photos and notes you submit for an appraisal, and the appraisals we create from them.
  • An account identifier. You start with an anonymous ID. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we also receive the Apple account identifier and the email Apple provides.
  • Purchase status, such as whether you have an active subscription. We never see your payment card.
  • Basic usage counts, such as how many scans you've used.

How we use it

To identify and value your items, keep your collection, enforce scan limits, provide purchases and support. We don't sell your data and we don't use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Anthropic, which runs the Claude AI model. Your photos and notes are sent to Anthropic to create each appraisal. The app asks for your permission before the first time this happens.
  • Google Firebase, which hosts our servers, database and photo storage.
  • RevenueCat, which manages subscriptions and in-app purchases.

Don't include people, documents or other sensitive information in your photos.

How long we keep it

We keep your collection and photos until you delete them or your account.

Your choices

Delete any item from its page. Delete your account and all data in the app under Settings, Delete account and data. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your data; contact us to use them.

Children

Heirloom isn't directed to children under 13.
