Support

Email SUPPORT_EMAIL and include the ID shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.

Cancel a subscription

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.

Restore a purchase

In the app, open Settings and tap Restore purchases.

Delete your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account and data. This removes your account, collection and photos.

Why does my estimate differ from what I was offered?

Estimates are automated, based on your photos and public sales data. They aren't certified appraisals, and real prices depend on condition, timing and the buyer.
